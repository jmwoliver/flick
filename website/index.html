Get started

  1. Install Flick on your Mac. In Terminal, run:
    curl -fsSL https://getflick.net/install.sh | bash
    The first release is coming soon. See what the script does.
  2. Get Flick for iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store. Until then, build it from source.
  3. Pair them. Open Flick on your iPhone, tap your Mac, and enter the PIN from Flick's menu bar icon.

What you can do

  • Use it as a trackpad. Click, right-click, drag and scroll, the same way you would on a MacBook.
  • Type. The keyboard opens when you click into a text field on your Mac. Autocorrect, swipe typing and dictation all work.
  • Switch apps. Your Mac's Dock is on your phone. Tap an app to bring it to the front.
  • Use it with one hand. Slide your thumb along the side edge to scroll, or along the bottom edge to switch Spaces. Swipe up from the bottom for Mission Control.
  • Use shortcuts. Tap ⌘C, ⌘V or your own shortcuts, with esc, tab and arrow keys always on hand.

Requires iOS 17 and macOS 14 or later. Works over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, with no account. Your phone and Mac talk only to each other. Privacy policy
