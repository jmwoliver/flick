Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Flick doesn't collect any data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Flick for iPhone and Flick for Mac talk only to each other, directly, over your local network or Bluetooth.

What the apps handle, and where it stays

Your input

Touches, clicks, scrolls and the text you type on the iPhone are sent to the Mac you paired with, so the Mac can perform them. This traffic is encrypted and never leaves your two devices.

Text in the field you're typing into

So the iPhone can show what you're editing, the Mac sends it the text around the cursor in the focused text field. Flick never reads or sends the contents of password fields. This text is kept only in memory while you use the app.

Your Mac's Dock

The Mac sends the iPhone the names and icons of apps in your Dock, so you can switch apps from the phone. Icons are cached on the iPhone.

Pairing

When you pair, the Mac and iPhone share a random security key, which each stores in its own Keychain. On iPhone it never leaves the device and isn't synced to iCloud. The iPhone also stores the name of the Mac it last used and a random identifier for this installation of the app, which it shares with the Mac. The Mac stores that identifier and the name of the paired iPhone.

Settings

Your preferences, such as pointer speed and pinned shortcuts, are stored on the device where you set them.

Performance log

Flick for Mac keeps a performance log on your Mac at ~/Library/Logs/Flick/perf.log, with timings and counts, plus which app and kind of text field had focus. It never contains the text you type. It's capped at about 10 MB, and you can delete it at any time.

Permissions

  • Local Network (iPhone and Mac): lets the iPhone find and connect to your Mac.
  • Bluetooth (iPhone and Mac): a second, low-latency path between the devices.
  • Accessibility (Mac): lets Flick move the pointer, type, and detect when a text field has focus.

None of these are used for anything else.

Third parties

Flick doesn't include third-party SDKs and doesn't share data with anyone. This website doesn't use cookies or analytics. It's hosted on Vercel, which, like any web host, processes standard request information such as IP addresses to serve pages.

Children

Flick doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Its full history is on GitHub.
