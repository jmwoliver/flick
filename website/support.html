Getting started

  1. Install Flick for Mac and open it. Allow Local Network access when asked.
  2. Turn on Accessibility for Flick: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.
  3. Click the Flick icon in the menu bar to see the 6-digit pairing PIN.
  4. Open Flick on your iPhone, tap your Mac, and enter the PIN.

You only need the PIN once. After that, your iPhone reconnects to that Mac on its own.

Troubleshooting

My Mac doesn't appear on the iPhone

Make sure Flick for Mac is running (look for its icon in the menu bar). Check that Local Network access is on for Flick on both devices: on iPhone, Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network; on Mac, System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. The devices work best on the same Wi-Fi network, but can also connect directly when they're close together.

It says "Wrong PIN" or "Couldn't pair"

Check the PIN in the Flick menu on your Mac and try again. The PIN changes when you click New PIN or Unpair. If the Mac's menu says pairing is paused, see the next question.

The Mac says "Pairing is paused"

After 5 wrong PINs, the Mac stops accepting pairing attempts for a short time, starting at 30 seconds, to protect against guessing. Wait for the pause to end, then try again with the PIN shown in the Mac's menu.

My iPhone can't reconnect, or says the Mac no longer recognizes it

The Mac was probably unpaired from this iPhone. On the iPhone, touch and hold your Mac in the list, choose Pair again with PIN, and enter the PIN from the Mac's menu.

It says the Mac is paired with another iPhone

Flick for Mac works with one iPhone at a time. To switch phones, click Unpair in the Flick menu on the Mac, then pair the new iPhone with the PIN.

The pointer doesn't move, or nothing gets typed

Flick for Mac needs Accessibility permission. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and turn on Flick. If it's already on, turn it off and on again; this can be needed after an update.

The keyboard doesn't appear in some apps

Flick detects text fields using the Mac's accessibility information. A few apps, such as some games and terminal emulators, don't provide it. Tap the keyboard button on the iPhone to open the keyboard yourself.

The pointer feels laggy on Wi-Fi

Flick sends every movement over both Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, so keep Bluetooth on for both devices. Macs on battery power can delay Wi-Fi traffic to save energy; plugging in often helps.
